space

space isn't an aspect i think about very often; i wouldn't say it holds relevance to my life, not as much as other aspects.

creation

i can create. anyone can. but most things that i make are pretty derivative, and i don't consider myself a "creative" person. i enjoy iterating on and fleshing out existing things more than i enjoy making completely "original" work because my imagination is fairly limited. but everything takes inspiration from something in the world of art, so i don't think my lack of creativity is anything special.

isolation

i connect with the detachment of the space aspect. isolation is something i'm familiar with, though a large portion of it is self-imposed rather than something i've been forced to deal with. there's a certain level of distance between me and other people that makes things feel more comfortable for myself.

propagation

i'm averse to the concept of propagation when it comes to myself. i don't want kids, mainly because i don't think i'd be a good parent as i am now, and i believe that bringing a being into life should be something that is done with extreme care and thought. creating life isn't something that should be done on a whim.

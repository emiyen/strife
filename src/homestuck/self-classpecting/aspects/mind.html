mind

mind is an aspect that a part of me feels daunted by, but i want to be able to embody and get better at utilizing it. it's an aspiration, in a way.

i'm often faced with choice paralysis in the face of having to make important decisions. i'm always wondering what the right thing to do is, whether it be during social interactions or making decisions that will change the course of my or other peoples' lives. some part of me is convinced there is a "right" decision or pathway in most situations, and that i simply lack the knowledge to make them.

i'm able to see a lot of different ways that i can go about things, but i'm held back by fear of "messing up" and "failing" or being faced by the consequences of a poorly thought out choice. i can put off decisions because they can bring me a great deal of anxiety, and if the anxiety is too great or a deadline is coming too fast for my brain to process and deliberate on the decision for, i just say fuck it we ball and make a decision just to make the decision.

in an ideal world, i would be more competent with honing in on decisions and actually taking action. i wouldn't be stuck in a state of constant deliberation, paralyzed by the weight of my choices. i'd take in all the data i have and use it to actually act instead of hoarding knowledge and doing nothing with it except turning it in my head in thought experiments.

as for the literal mind, i have a lot of fascination with it; how people think, why people do the things they do. it's what drew me to things like "personality type" systems as well as actual psychology and sociology. i want to be able to understand people—to create a framework or blueprint for me to navigate the world and my interactions with people. i absolutely adore learning more about the ways other people function. the world is full of so many different and intriguing minds, and i'd love to be able to crack them open and dig my fingers in for some hands-on learning.

overall, mind is an aspect i feel a lot of connection to. it pervades every part of my life, after all, and directly correlates with the things i'm always worrying about.

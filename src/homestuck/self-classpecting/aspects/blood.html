blood

blood—what it represents, at least—feels like a burden to me. which sounds bad, considering blood is all about relationships and unity and bonds. what the hell, man.

i've always struggled in connecting with people and feeling a sense of unity. my interpersonal relationships have had their issues, and i have a tendency to drift away from people and have historically made decisions to cut off relationships too eagerly, believing that things are unsalvageable or simple not being willing to put in the effort to maintain a relationship. i always feel like i need to watch for the other shoe to drop in my relationships, and am often plagued with the feeling that none of my relationships will last, and thus i should mentally prepare myself for their inevitable ends.

all that relates to being blood-bound does not come naturally to me. i often feel like a newborn fawn stumbling on wobbly legs whenever i try to navigate relationships. or perhaps a small toddler stumbling around in the dark and bumping into fucking everything. the thing is, i know what it takes to maintain relationships and allow them to thrive. it's putting it into practice that i have the most trouble with, primarily due to fear, and the feeling that i am unable to embody the ideals i have relative to relationships.

to sum up my thoughts on blood: relationships are everything. they are perhaps the most integral part of human society with us being an extremely social species. it takes a lot of upkeep to be able to maintain relationships, and i don't think i have what it takes to be able to "be good" at relationships. though maybe it'd be more accurate to say i'm terrified to try, leading to me being quite stingy and withdrawn.

my relationship to blood as a whole can be summarized with one word: inadequacy.

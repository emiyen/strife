time

short answer: i never have enough of this shit.

slightly longer answer: i struggle with time. i'm pretty time-blind, and it often slips through my fingers because i get absorbed in something or other and then lose track of it. i often feel like i'm running out of time to do the things i want and love to do. but of course, it's not really that i'm lacking in time; i'm just horrible at managing mine. it's as if i can't keep up with the endless, unstoppable march. if i could have the power to pause time indefinitely so i could do the things i wanted to do, i would take it in a heartbeat.

rage

i don't have enough rage or fury. i compulsively suppress anger, as i don't really feel comfortable with expressing the emotion, though i've had my angry moments. this means i have a tendency to lean more towards inertia rather than active change and upheavals, as rage is just as powerful a driving force as hope is. in truth, i have a deficit of both driving forces, making me someone prone to stagnation.

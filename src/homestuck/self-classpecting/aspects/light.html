light

knowledge

fuck me, i love knowledge. get me ten more of those.

i feel like i'm constantly hungry for knowledge. knowledge is something i take great pleasure in accumulating and hoarding. the direction my hunger for knowledge goes varies, though, and it isn't always useful. i simply love the act of diving down rabbit holes to learn more about things. learning is like crack to me, given i'm interested in the topic.

knowledge is most powerful for informing decisions, but i don't often put that into action. i'm more of a... collect all of it and then just be happy that i have it kind of guy. though my favorite thing to do with knowledge is to share it when i find it genuinely useful. i think informing people, and giving them the tools to further their own knowledge, is wonderful.

one primary example i have is regarding identity and the self. self-knowledge is particularly important to me, and i enjoy helping others towards greater self-understanding. it's why i love the enneagram as a framework for understanding one's subconscious motivations and drives. it's an excellent tool for personal growth, given it's used properly.

relevance

compared to knowledge, this is a part of light that i really don't care for. i think relevance is dangerous. i don't want to be in the spotlight or the center of attention. i don't want to be the talk of the town. that shit's scary, get me outta there.

fortune/luck

i'm not really a believer in luck as a power. as in, i don't think it exists. i don't think there's a way of increasing your luck, only increasing your odds through manipulation of your circumstances and environment.

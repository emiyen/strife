hope

i'm envious of people who have a lot of hope. i think the concept of having the utmost faith in something, truly believing in something, is amazing. it's powerful. ideals, dreams, aspirations; these are things that keep people going and fuel innovation.

i used to wish i believed in a god. i used to wish i had belief in something, anything, to anchor myself to. but i find myself plagued by constant questions and unable to commit myself to any particular belief. i'm pretty agnostic, and try to keep things open.

i've had a definitive lack of hope for the majority of my life, and i have little to faith in most things except for the idea that anything and everything is unreliable and subject to change.

overall, hope is an aspect that i feel very distant from, but one that i also look up to. i very sincerely doubt it's my aspect, but i think the hope-bound are pretty damn sick.

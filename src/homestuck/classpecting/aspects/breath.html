breath

freedom

i have a weird relationship with the concept of freedom. there isn't much that shackles me down physically, and i have plenty of opportunities in life to do what i want. it's the limitations i impose upon myself that tie me down. i often feel like i'm bound by obligations and the expectations people have of me. it's only when i'm alone that i really feel free of those weights. if i had to make it into a metaphor, i rarely feel like i have enough room to breathe when i'm with other people. it often feels like i'm always yearning for freedoms that are unattainabel and unrealistic—i will always be bound down to something.

change

i'm distinctly afraid of change and quite slothful, so i often avoid grabbing opportunities with my own two hands. change is something that i have a tendency to inch towards, as opposed to launching myself full force at it. i used to consider myself flexible, but after reflection i've come to realize that i'm pretty stubborn and inflexible, and have a tendency to dig in my heels in the face of changes. i'm a creature of comfort, routine, familiarity.

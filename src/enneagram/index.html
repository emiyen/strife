what is it

the enneagram is often described as a personality type system, though the accuracy of that depends on what you consider to be your "personality". at its core, the enneagram highlights the fears and fixations people have acquired as a result of childhood experiences and wounds. according to enneagram theory, these wounds and the coping mechanisms/adaptations you've made to accomodate for them are what make up your "false personality". as a result of learning some kind of message through childhood and adolescence, your attention grew narrowed towards certain fixations and passions. you took on certain behavior patterns to adjust and have your needs met.

there are nine enneagram types (numbered 1 through 9), each with their own sets of beliefs about the world, fears, fixations, and virtues. no type is better than another; each have their faults.

important to note is that enneagram is not a science. any system that attempts to categorize the human experience into a limited number of boxes will inevitably fall short in one way or another. i simply take what is useful for my own personal needs and growth.

what's the point

the best way to use the enneagram, in my opinion, is as a framework for personal growth. take what you feel you need to hear, use it to curate your awareness of your maladaptive behavior patterns, and use that awareness to circumvent those behaviors and move towards healthier ones. easier said than done, but learning your enneagram type is really only one minor step in the larger scheme of learning about yourself.

where can i learn more

i've compiled a list of resources that i personally used to familiarize myself with the enneagram. feel free to look around

i highly recommend going through as many sources as possible to gain a fuller, more personalized understanding of the system. information about the enneagram is scattered around various different sources—there is no singular centralized reliable source, unfortunately, as the enneagram has many different lenses you can view it from.

practicing trying to type characters and people will flesh out your knowledge of the enneagram. talk with other people about it, especially those who may have more insight to share on the topic

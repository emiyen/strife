on the night of 09 oct 2026 (or maybe it bled past midnight and into the 10th) i had a string of realizations. during it, i was contemplating making a character based off of a part of myself; the part that wants to be more spontaneous and act from my gut more often. i like to take pieces of myself and turn them into characters by amplifying certain traits or faults, and this was the next mental project i was entertaining.

i talked with some people who said this character could be either a page of hope or breath—two canon classpects of which we have concrete examples for the arcs of. it got me thinking specifically about jake english, which then led to me realizing just how relatable he is as a character, something i'd never allowed myself to consider.

and then my thoughts veered deeply into the deep end with "no wonder why i hate myself." wow, way to kill the vibe.

growing up, my homestuck social circle was often pretty jake-negative. or at least, not jake-positive. i guess a part of me had introjected that feeling.* i've always viewed the traits that i share with jake as negative. and i mean... they are pretty negative. i relate the most to his avoidance and passivity in the main story of homestuck—how shit just happens to him, rather than him being an active participant in his own life. a lot of people just view him as quite boring. helpess, and incompetent. and i guess i view myself as that way, too—incompetent most of all.

another reason i'd never considered jake as a relatable character until now: i'd been fixated primarily on the striders for the entire time i'd been into homestuck. i still am, honestly. but as is the case for many, my "relating" to them wasn't entirely because i felt like they were like me—it was because i wanted to be like them. my younger self viewed them as the pinnacles of guyhood for one reason or another, and i wanted that badly for myself. it led to me casting aside most of the other human characters when it came to viewing them with the same level of depth and feeling that i did with the striders, who i projected heavily onto. in truth, i'm nothing like either of them.

in short, i'd ignored the very relatable qualities of jake english because i didn't like myself at the time and preferred to fit myself into the skin of characters i did like. characters that were more competent, more active.

the start of this realization about myself wasn't just the character i was planning on creating. it really started when i got page of mind on a classpecting quiz several days prior. it was the first time i really connected with a classpect that i've gotten assigned. i found this to be very unfortunate.

pages as a class embody the potential man meme. they're an incredibly slow-burn but high-potential class that starts off very weak and often relying on others, often ineffectively misusing their aspect to make things easier on themselves. they're meant to rise up to the challenge in order to reach their full potential. canon characters (really just vriska) dog on the page class for being weak weenie hut jr. type shit.

in retrospect, this spiel about pages really reflects my views on myself: someone who is mentally weak and unable to—or too afraid to—rise up to the challenges they're faced with, clinging onto false notions of having "potential" in order to feel better about themself. pretty bleak. but i guess getting a page class result that i connected with really cracked open my head with regards to receptiveness to accepting my jake english-ness, because here i am now, doing that.

expectations and potential haunt me. i'm well aware of my "potential" as a person and the expectations people have of me because of that potential, and it only serves to act as additional pressure that makes me want to run and hide. i'm going to get complainy, maybe even whiny here for a second, so bear with me:

i don't want to do great things. i don't want to reach my "highest potential". i simply want to exist and do things i enjoy in peace, outside of the spotlight and any sort of relevance. i'm far from an achievement chaser, rather being a chronic underachiever, because i'm afraid to try—afraid to fail. i'm probably as far as you can get from vriska, the type 3, as possible. it's part of why i've been considering E9 for my core enneagram type as opposed to E5.

but that's it, isn't it—the challenge of the page class. to overcome the roadblocks keeping them from achieving greatness. my hesitancy to rise up to the challenge only serves as further evidence of my pagehood. it's quite similar to tavros's start, actually, which i viewed with an automatic "yikes!" due to introjecting similar beliefs about him as i did with jake. but truly, the projected arc of the page class is all too fitting for what i view to be the arc i have to take to self-actualize.

that's about it for my jake english and page class reflections relative to myself.

i think when i eventually reread homestuck, i want to take more care to giving space in my head for characters other than the striders, particularly jake now that i've grown more accepting of the fact that i share a lot of qualities with him as a page. another character i want to pay more attention to is rose lalonde, who i also relate to for a couple of different reasons.

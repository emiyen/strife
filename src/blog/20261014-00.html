cw: weed

i'm gonna be quitting regular THC use indefinitely (potentially forever?). mainly because the drawbacks (interfering with my sleep schedule) outweigh the benefits (temporary relaxation and relief from general and social anxiety). not really something i'm happy about, but the interference with sleep has been a pretty big issue that's led to me sleeping most of the day away, which isn't ideal either.

in place of it my psych prescribed me hydroxyzine pamoate for anxiety symptoms since it's not habit-forming. i looked up a bit about the medication and it seems to be benadryl on steroids, so for the first time i'll do it either on a day where i have no obligations or at night. i'm hoping to get at least some minor anxiety relief from it. if not, we'll just have to try something else.

i've been trying to put a finger on why i like getting high in the first place to see what i'm missing from my life so i can fill it with other things:

mental flexibility
my brain is able to go places it usually wouldn't naturally drift towards. i find this helps with reflecting and creative thinking. while my thoughts tend to be scattered while i'm high, i can organize them when i'm sober so long as i record them somewhere. historically, this has helped me with coming to realizations about myself, particular things i might avoid while i'm sober.
pleasure
this one's pretty simple. brain gets happy chemicals, d gets happy. things tend to feel good/better when i'm high.
relaxation / anxiety relief
i feel like i'm able to get out of my head for once. it's nice to be able to wind down, as opposed to feeling like my obligations and future are constantly lurking in the back of my head. it's an escape, but it also helps me feel like i can exist in my body, in the present, eradicating my worries about the future for a little while.
socialization
i feel the need to socialize more keenly when i'm high, which in my case is a good thing, because i tend to be an asocial little bastard. in combination with the anxiety relief, which includes my social anxiety, it often leads to me being more talkative—online, at least. i feel lonelier, but i view that as me becoming more in tune with my loneliness and my social needs, as opposed to the THC creating a feeling of loneliness out of nothing.

in short and unsurprisingly, the feel-good drug feels good. but it is causing problems, namely with sleep, which is then causing problems with my productivity as a student. i'm at a period in my life where i really need to lock in and get my shit together, so i can't afford that. in the hands of someone with better self-discipline it probably wouldn't be an issue, but i struggle with coping. i think if i had the choice i'd be high a lot more frequently than i already get, which tended to be nightly pre-quitting.

this isn't my first time quitting THC. back when i was 16 my parents found out i was vaping it and as a result was under constant watch and suspicion. i had to stop for several months before i found an alternative way to get my hands on some carts. so this isn't something i'm new to. and i've quit voluntarily before. i just find myself circling back to THC when i feel a greater need for the ability to relax. and unfortunately, my stress tolerance is quite low.

maybe one day i'll return to using THC recreationally. preferrably when i'm in a better place for it mentally, without risk of sparking dependence.

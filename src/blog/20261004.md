**cw: weed, magic mushrooms**

i love observing people bro. it's so interesting just watching people have conversations in discord servers and seeing how different people interact with each other. there's something about it that scratches an itch in my brain. so i guess i do like people after all—just from a distance.

there's something so appealing and comfortable about being able to be completely invisible but still being able to "interact" with others with my technical presence. there are no expectations for me to react or respond. i get my fill of "social interaction". i can drop in at random if i feel and spark up a conversation. it makes for a comfortable social environment for me compared to in-person interactions.

i am high as hell while i'm writing this though, so maybe this is nonsensical or creepy.

been trying to write a fic where dirk strider gets high for the first time on weed. and then maybe ill translate my own experience w psilocybin onto him in another fic or a second chapter. i've been kinda struggling though because whenever i keep getting too high to continue writing it. (though the reason i was getting high was to make sure my writing of the experience was accurate. but now i get stuck trying to translate the experience into words.)

i really love seeing how characters who are typically really high-strung or uptight are like while under the influence of substances. is this, perhaps. gap moe.

there's something so delightful about the feeling of being seen and labeled. i can appreciate it more when i'm high and thus actually social/feeling the need to interact with people. but i understand why so many people want to feel like they're understood, and sometimes labels do a good job of helping with that. it's all quite human. 

i love getting high. i love watching to see how the wire mesh of my brain starts getting tangled. it's just so fascinating to be able to observe my own reactions, the things i do when things get loopy up there, how i change if at all or if i'm just getting a deeper look into myself and what's already there that i don't pay attention to.

i love learning to understand people better. it's such a great feeling. talking about the enneagram today was very fun.

i get so terrified of expressing myself while i can still be seen, at least in the moment. it's so much more comfortable being unseen. i love to control what it is that people see about me because it gives me a sense of security. everything i reveal should be a choice.

i wish i could be more comfortable directly interacting with people while sober.

maybe i should wait until i'm sober to prune this blog post and make it more presentable, but i also want evidence in the morning of the ways my brain decided to bend. i wish i did the same while i was high on psilocybin, but all i have left of memory of that experience is in the form of a retelling of what happened that i did a little while after the actual high. i gotta start writing trip reports more often or writing/blogging while high more often to see what directions my head goes. i love seeing myself unfiltered.

i was thinking about coding earlier, and how i love coding. particularly html/css. i'm learning a lot of stuff out of order and definitely not getting all of the fundamentals/foundations, but it's really fucking fun to hit a wall and have to research and experiment to find out how to fix a thing or do a thing i'm trying to do. it makes my brain happy. it's like giving it exercise if i actually liked exercise. it's just a puzzle that scratches an itch for this simple kind of mental exercise. i've been told this is apparently par for the course for someone who is mind-bound.

speaking of which; after many years i've finally found a classpect that i feel actually fits me. page of mind. shoutout to the [sburbio classpect quiz](https://sburbio-teste-de-classpect.vercel.app/). note: you can change the site language with the little dropdown menu next to the class test link in the navbar. i feel like it fits me as a person, which was an exciting result. i've been wondering about my classpect for many years now, so it's comforting finally landing on something. i suppose this is a consequence of growing out of my teenage years and into an adult, mentally. a more stable sense of self. god i'm so glad i'm out of my teen years, jesus christ.

kind of weird but i wish i could clone myself so i could observe what i was like from a third-person perspective. i couldn't in good conscience do that, even to another me, but the thought is definitely appealing to my curiosity.

maybe like i'm narcissus. obsessed with my own reflection. i'm certainly not in love with it, but i love to observe myself as if i were someone else entirely, in an almost obsessive manner, like a scientist observing something under a microscope and taking notes. i've entertained thoughts of wishing i were a fictional character so i could analyze myself. what a self obsessed mf am i right??

i feel like it's not as weird or creepy as if i put someone else under the microscope though.

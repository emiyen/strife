--- title: "starting kgtac, self-classpecting, & other thoughts" tags: - high - homestuck ---

cw: weed

i started reading karkat goes to a convention and i've been really enjoying it so far. it's bringing me back similar feelings to when i was reading homestuck proper for the first time. it kind of feels like coming home. unfortunately, i'm too high to continue reading and actually remember and take in events of the story, so i'll be saving continuing reading for tomorrow.

i haven't been fixated on homestuck for a little while so it's definitely nice to be back and see the community is thriving just as strongly as ever, and with newgens too. to top it off. some people are even as old as when i was initially reading homestuck while it was still an ongoing work. that's crazy. i feel like an oldhead and i'm only 23 at the moment i'm writing this.

my mind is all over the place as a result of my inebriation, but i think the strongest effects are starting to dissipate, so my head is just barely clear enough to write coherent thoughts down again. my first draft of this blog post was significantly more messy.

i've been contemplating my classpect again. i thought i was settled with page of mind after the sburbio classpect quiz that gave me that result, but i just can't help but undermine and be suspicious of things i guess, especially when it comes to myself and my self-perception. my sense of identity is much stronger and more stable than it used to be several years ago, but i still lack certainty in myself and my judgement. and i certainly don't have a good enough understanding of classpects in order to make judgements about my own right now.

i'm re-evaluating my relationship to heart. while the sburbio quiz results said i got -10 points for heart, i'm still inclined to give heart some weight in the game of "what's my classpect?" i guess maybe i'm just not convinced that i'm that far away from it, even if i lean towards mind as an aspect.

developing a better understanding of myself is one of my biggest goals in life right now. i talked about it in a much more rambly and nonsensical blog post than this one.

however. i think i feel more comfortable dissecting myself as a foreign entity more than i feel comfortable actually being myself. there's comfort in the level of distance i can put between myself and... myself. as if it'll protect me from my own lowly self-perception.

i love the idea of being someone else. i drown in escapism even when the life i'm escaping to is objectively worse than my own.

side note: getting high has been kind of awful for my sleep lately, but wonderful for the sake of writing out my thoughts. it gets the gears in my head turning instead of stuk trying to censor or correct myself before i've even finished a thought.
